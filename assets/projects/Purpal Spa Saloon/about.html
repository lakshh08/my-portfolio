A little about us

Beauty with a personal touch

Purpal Spa Saloon is a place to feel welcomed, listened to and cared for—one visit at a time.

Our story

Created for your time to feel like yours

We imagine a salon where beauty and wellbeing come together in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere. Whether you're visiting for a quick refresh or a longer self-care moment, our intention is to help you feel comfortable and confident.

Our story is still being written alongside the people who walk through our doors. We invite you to tell us what matters to you and make your visit your own.

Our mission

Care that starts with listening

To offer a welcoming, thoughtful salon experience where each guest can explore beauty and wellness services with care, comfort and respect.

Our vision

A little more confidence, every day

To be a trusted neighborhood destination for personal care—known for warm hospitality, attentive service and a calm place to recharge.

Our philosophy

Your preferences come first

Beauty is personal. We believe a great visit begins with understanding what makes you feel good—your routine, your comfort and the results you're hoping for.

  • Take time to discuss your preferences
  • Make space for a calm, welcoming visit
  • Offer clear information about services and sample pricing
  • Respect that every guest's idea of feeling beautiful is their own
